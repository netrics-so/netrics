# Milestone 11.2 — App slides on TV

## Outcome

An Apple TV shows an app portfolio as a rotating dashboard: an overview
slide, then one slide per app with the app's icon. A paid app's slide shows
revenue, downloads, ratings, the latest review and the visitors of the app's
website; a free app's slide leaves revenue out. Every number says when
netrics fetched it, which day it covers, and when the provider will publish
the next numbers. The cadence differs per kind of data: Apple publishes a
day's sales in the morning Pacific Time of the next day, reviews arrive
continuously, Search Console is two to three days behind. netrics also
fetches new numbers soon after they are published instead of on a fixed
six-hour interval.

The target is the hosted service and a netrics TV TestFlight build.
GitHub milestone "11.2 App slides on TV".

## Dependencies

- Milestone 09 (Dashboard Studio): slides, widgets, logos, resource icons.
- Milestone 11 (reusable data models): the App Portfolio template, its
  product identity and measure widgets.
- Milestone 09.1: every new string in English and German.

## In scope

- Data freshness (ADR 0025): connectors report per stream the last
  complete day and when the next period is expected; the host stores it,
  times syncs to it and passes it to every widget and device
- Freshness on web screens and in netrics TV, including measure widgets
- Slide scope: a slide set to one app, with its own header logo
- App identity in the template: icon, paid or free (automatic from
  proceeds, owner override), linked website
- App Portfolio template v4: overview plus one slide per app, paid and free
  layouts, update from v3
- A netrics TV TestFlight build with these features

## Out of scope

- Google Play review text (Play has no review ingestion yet) — follow-up
- More than 12 slides per dashboard, or playlists of several dashboards
- App Store submission of netrics TV (#419)
- Self-hosted acceptance (stays with #58 and #420)

## Exit gate

shows the overview and one slide per app of the owner's App Store Connect
account, free apps in the free layout, and every number with its retrieval

appear within about an hour of Apple publishing them.
